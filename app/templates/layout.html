SYSTEM GUIDE: ECONOMY

XP & PROGRESSION

• Each level needs a little more XP than the last (Lv 1: 160 XP, Lv 15: 2,760, Lv 50: 14,450, Lv 100: 37,960). Rewards are fixed, so early levels fly by and later ones take dedication.

• An active player (dailies, an hour or two of focus, a couple of quests) reaches Level 100 in roughly 2 Years.

  • Quests: E 40 · D 80 · C 150 · B 300 · A 600 XP. Non-daily B and A grow with you (at least 5% / 15% of your level). S-Rank = instant level up (1/week).
  • Dailies: each daily pays its rank XP, plus +500 XP when all are done.
  • Void Focus: 600 XP / hour of deep work.
  • Bonuses: INT (+5% per 10), class bonuses, titles and sleep condition boost your XP (but not league points, which use base XP so the league stays fair).

CURRENCIES

  • Coins (C): Used exclusively for System Shop artifacts. Earned via Void Focus (10/hr) and B+ Rank Quests. Endgame gear costs up to 15,000 C.
  • Gold (G): Earned by completing all dailies (100/day) and from B+ Rank Quests. Spend it on real-life rewards you price yourself in the Shop (My Rewards) or a "Night Out" (600G). Gold is halved every Monday at 1:00 AM, so you can save, but not hoard.

ATTRIBUTES (LIFE AREAS)

• Every level gives +1 to all stats. Clearing all your dailies also grants 3 points to allocate yourself on the dashboard.

  • Strength: Physical fitness, gym consistency, and diet adherence.
  • Intelligence: University work, coding, reading, and learning.
  • Agility: Speed of task completion and defeating procrastination.
  • Sense: Meditation, socializing, and networking.
  • Vitality: Sleep consistency, health, and energy preservation.

THE PENALTY SYSTEM

• Dailies reset at 1:00 AM. Missing them for 1 day applies a -20% Stat Debuff (reduced by STR) and glitches your UI.

• Missing 2 days in a row enters the Penalty Zone: your UI turns red and a Survival Quest is issued. Completing it (or all your dailies) clears the penalty.

• Miss its deadline and you take on XP debt (half a level, capped at one level): new XP pays it off before filling your bar. You never lose levels or stats.

• {{ icon('snowflake', 'icon-frost') }} Streak Freezes: earn one every 7-day streak (max 2). A missed day uses one automatically: no streak loss, no debuff, no penalty progress.

• {{ icon('skull', 'icon-danger') }} Hardcore Mode (Settings, opt-in): +10% XP and a badge, but a failed Penalty Quest costs one level instead of XP debt.

RECOVERY & SLEEP

• The system monitors your physical condition.

  • Well Rested: Log 7.5+ hours of sleep to gain a 1.1x (+10%) XP multiplier.
  • Normal: 6 to 7.5 hours. No modifier.
  • Tired: Sleeping less than 6 hours breaks your sleep streak and applies a 0.5x (-50%) XP multiplier until you log a better night.
  • Use the Elixir of Life from the shop to cure fatigue instantly.

FRIENDS & DAILY LIFE

  • Raids: shared goals (e.g. 100 km together). Clear them in time: every contributor gets +500 XP, +100 Coins.
  • Hall of Feats: cheer friends' S-rank clears with 🔥 👏 💪 👑 on the Leaderboard.
  • Your clock: resets follow your timezone. Set it and your daily reminder time in Settings.
  • Install: add Shadow OS to your home screen to use it like an app.

GATES & LOOT

• Gate progress counts automatically from your quests, focus, dailies and sleep. Nothing extra to log. Gate rewards never add league points.

  • {{ icon('swords') }} Gates (Lv 15): one appears every week. Enter when you're ready, then clear two objectives and a boss within 72 hours for bonus XP (a quarter level), Coins and an item.
  • {{ icon('triangle-alert') }} Red Gates (Lv 30): rare surprises. Enter within 24 hours, clear within 48. Harder, but double rewards and Rare-or-better loot. Declining or failing costs nothing.
  • {{ icon('gem') }} Loot: drops from Gates, and sometimes from non-daily B, A and S quests. Common and Rare from Lv 15, Epic from 30, Legendary from 60. Duplicates are salvaged into Coins.
  • {{ icon('crown') }} Legendary sets: 2 pieces give +5 to two stats, 4 pieces give +5% XP. From Lv 80 every Red Gate drops a Legendary piece.

HUNTER RANK

• Ranks D and C come automatically (Lv 10 / 20 with enough total stats). Promotions to B, A and S (Lv 40 / 60 / 80) need a 7-day Rank Assessment that opens once you qualify. Fail it and you can retry 3 days later.

TITLES & SHADOWS

• Titles: Unlocked by level, streaks, stats and wealth (e.g., "The One Who Overcame Adversity"). The equipped title grants its bonus (XP, Coins or stats). Tap your title under your name to see them all.

• The Shadow Army: Clearing an S-Rank quest lets you "Arise" it into a shadow, granting a permanent, stacking +1% to all stats (boosted by SNS). Unclaimed shadows wait in ARMY.

• Shadow ranks (Lv 25): in ARMY, assign a shadow to a quest. Every completion is a kill: 10 make it a Knight, 30 Elite, 60 Commander, 100 Marshal. Each grade multiplies its share of the army buff.

ASCENSION

• At Level 100 you can Ascend: back to Level 1, keeping your class, skill, rank, stats, gear, shadows and titles, plus a permanent +5% XP for every Ascension.

• See every unlock on the Road to Monarch.
